## Create your site

Open `/admin/sites` and create a site with your store name and a unique address.
Your site starts as a private draft. Confirm your company details, contact address
and navigation before inviting visitors.

---

## Edit pages and media

Choose a page in the workspace. Edit its sections, reorder blocks and preview the
result. Use the media library for images and alternative text. Shared settings
control your logo, colours, navigation and footer. Save a draft before publishing.

---

## Build with chat

Give the builder a clear business description and the pages you need. Review the
proposed changes in the same draft preview used by the classical editor.

> Business: Northstar Goods sells everyday accessories.
> Audience: People looking for useful goods for work and travel.
> Pages: Home, Shop, About, Contact and Journal.
> Tone: Clear, friendly and practical. Use fictional sample details.
> Change the selected hero draft only. Do not publish.

Check product facts and references yourself. Use undo to reverse an unwanted change.
Without a model key, the builder offers a guided preset wizard.

---

## Manage products

Create products, variants and collections in the catalogue. Enter prices in the
chosen currency, review stock and attach product images. Keep unavailable products
private until their details are ready. Approve only authentic customer reviews.

---

## Prepare commerce

Set your delivery regions, shipping rates and tax configuration. Store payment
credentials belong to one site. Global Stripe or PayPal keys are used only when
an operator explicitly configures the corresponding primary-site slug.

Use the private simulator to explore checkout, subscriptions and customer accounts.
Local simulation does not prove provider acceptance. Complete your own payment,
email and fulfilment checks before taking orders.

---

## Review and launch

Review desktop and mobile previews, legal policies, product claims and all pending
placeholders. The compliance scanner checks wording before publication; merchant
review is still required. Confirm contact delivery and provider setup, then use
the go-live review to publish the approved site.

See [the merchant editing guide](PHASE1_MERCHANT_GUIDE.md) and
[the commerce operations guide](PHASE2_SANDBOX_OPERATIONS.md).
