## 3. Design direction

**Feel:** clean, modern, stylish, fresh and energetic, premium but approachable. Similar in spirit to drinkh2tab.com, carmount.com and hydroxy.health – **not a copy**. Do not reproduce their layouts, images or text.

**The site must NOT feel AI-generated.** Avoid: purple or rainbow gradients, glowing orbs, glassmorphism everywhere, identical rounded cards repeated in endless grids, generic stock-looking icon sets, centred-everything layouts, and filler copy such as "unlock your potential". Prefer: generous whitespace, real editorial typography, asymmetric layouts, large confident imagery, purposeful motion.

**Logo:** the logo is black and blue, which can feel masculine. Use it as an accent, not as the whole palette.
- Files provided: black-text version (h24you_must, PNG and SVG) for light backgrounds; white-text version (h24you_valge, PNG and SVG) for use over dark video or dark sections. Prefer SVG.
- The blue in the logo runs from bright cyan-blue (about #14A5EE) to deep blue (about #0A3DC7).

**Suggested palette (adjust if it looks better, but keep the principle):**
- Base: warm off-white (#FAFAF7) and white.
- Text: near-black (#0E1116), soft grey for secondary text.
- Accent: the logo blue, used sparingly for buttons, links and small highlights.
- Support tones: very pale aqua (#E8F4FB) and one soft warm neutral (e.g. sand #EFE8DE) for section backgrounds.
- **The site as a whole must be light and airy, not black-and-blue.** Dark sections only where the video or a single feature block needs them.

**Fonts:** headings in **Lovera**, body text in **Quicksand**. If Lovera is not available under a free licence in Shopify, tell me and use a close alternative until I supply the font file. Keep the type scale clear: large headings, comfortable body size (16–18px), generous line height.

**Imagery and motion:** all photos and videos are **PLACEHOLDERS** (see section 9).

**Energy and enthusiasm.** The site should feel alive and make people curious and eager to try the product. Create that energy with these tools, none of which need a health claim:
- **Sensory imagery:** macro shots of bubbles, tablets dissolving into clear water, slow-motion pours, condensation on a glass, fresh fruit for the flavours. Show the moment of the first sip.
- **Ritual and lifestyle:** the morning glass, the gym bag, the desk, the kitchen at sunrise. Let people picture the habit.
- **Motion with purpose:** counters that count up when they scroll into view, bubbles rising in section transitions, smooth video moments, hover effects on product cards. Keep it smooth and light, never busy.
- **Colour:** let the logo blue pop with confidence on buttons, highlights and key headlines, and use fresh flavour-inspired accent tints (soft raspberry pink, pineapple yellow) sparingly on the flavour selectors and product cards. The base stays light.
- **Copy rhythm:** confident headlines, short sentences, an occasional playful line, clear calls to action ("Try your first glass", "Explore the research"). Frame the research as a discovery: "2,000+ published articles. Still being explored." Only use facts from section 7.
- **Curiosity hooks:** "What is molecular hydrogen?", "See what researchers are studying", "How does a tablet become hydrogen water?" – each links to a Learn or Science page.
- **Social proof:** a strong reviews section, with room for customer photos later.
- **Small delights:** a subtle first-order offer, a satisfying add-to-cart animation, a friendly empty-cart message.

## 8. Compliance and wording rules (very important)

This is a dietary supplement and a bottle sold in the US. Follow these rules everywhere on the site, including blog posts and alt text.

- Never state or imply that a product diagnoses, treats, cures or prevents any disease. Do not name diseases or conditions (for example diabetes, Parkinson's, cancer, arthritis) in connection with a product.
- Prefer research language: "Researchers have studied…", "Studies have looked at…", "Early research suggests…", "More research is needed."
- Benefit wording about the body's normal function (structure and function statements) may only use the **approved benefit statements** listed below, each with an asterisk and the FDA disclaimer in the same view. Do not create new benefit statements yourself.
- Do not use "proven", "clinically proven", "guaranteed", "detox", "anti-aging" or numeric health-benefit claims. Do not present any research finding as settled fact.
- The Science and Learn pages link to sources and let people read them. They should not contain product buy buttons inside the study lists.
- No invented reviews, testimonials, statistics, awards, certifications or medical endorsements. If a fact is missing, write a visible PLACEHOLDER.
- Have the final wording checked by a US regulatory adviser before launch.
